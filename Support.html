Frequently asked questions

Is XM Mint free?
The base version of XM Mint is free. Optional premium features, if any, will be clearly labelled in-app.
Does XM Mint connect to my brokerage?
No. XM Mint is a manual tracker — you add your holdings, transactions and prices yourself. It does not connect to live markets, exchanges or brokerages.
Where is my data stored?
All data is stored locally on your device, inside the App's sandbox. It is not synced to the cloud. Use JSON Export in Settings to back up.
How do I back up my portfolio?
Open Settings → Data → Export JSON. Save the resulting file to your Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or another location of your choice.
How do I move my data to a new device?
Export JSON on the old device, then import the same file on the new device from Settings → Data → Import JSON.
How do I delete my data?
Open Settings → Data → Reset / Clear all data. Deleting the App also removes all locally stored data.
Does XM Mint track me?
No. See the Tracking Declaration on the Home page and the Privacy Policy for full details.
Can XM Mint give me financial advice?
No. XM Mint is an educational and personal-tracking tool only. Always verify positions against your official brokerage statement.

Known limitations

  • Price refresh depends on the availability of the public data source; outages may cause stale values.
  • XM Mint does not stream real-time tick data.
  • Currency conversion uses the rates bundled with the App or last refreshed rates; FX is informational only.
