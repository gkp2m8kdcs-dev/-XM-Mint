Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026 · App name: XM Mint · Developer contact: huelmelissa1841991@gmail.com

Short version: XM Mint does not track you. No analytics, no advertising, no account, no cloud sync. Your portfolio data stays on your device.

1. Overview

XM Mint ("the App", "we", "our") is an on-device investment portfolio tracker. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using XM Mint you agree to the practices described below.

2. Tracking Declaration

XM Mint does not perform any tracking as defined under the Apple App Store "Tracking" policy (AppTrackingTransparency framework) or under applicable privacy laws such as GDPR or ePrivacy.

Specifically, the App does not:

  • Access or read your device advertising identifier (IDFA), Identifier for Vendors (IDFV), Google Advertising ID (GAID), or any similar device identifier.
  • Use fingerprinting techniques (combining device, browser, OS or IP-derived attributes) to identify you across apps or services.
  • Share, sell, or rent any personal information to data brokers, advertising networks, attribution platforms, or analytics vendors.
  • Display any advertising or sponsored content inside the App.
  • Perform cross-app or cross-website tracking.
  • Use cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, SDKs, or local storage identifiers for tracking purposes.

Because XM Mint does not track you, the App does not display the iOS "Allow tracking" prompt. If we ever add a feature that would qualify as tracking, we will request your explicit permission through AppTrackingTransparency before any such activity begins, update this Privacy Policy, and surface a clear in-app notice.

3. Data stored on your device

The following data is created and stored locally inside the App's private sandbox on your device only:

  • Holdings, transactions, cost basis, fees, notes and tags you enter.
  • Goals, planned contributions, cash-flow entries and currency preferences.
  • Risk questionnaire answers and computed risk profile.
  • Photos you attach to holdings or transactions (camera or photo library).
  • Exported JSON backups you choose to save or share.

This data is processed on-device. The developer does not have remote access to it, and it is not transmitted to any server we operate. Deleting the App permanently removes this data.

4. Permissions requested

  • Camera — only when you tap the camera button to attach a receipt or asset image. Captured images are stored in the App's sandbox.
  • Photo Library (read) — only when you choose to attach an existing image. We use the standard iOS picker, which returns a single item to the App.
  • Photo Library (write/add) — only when you explicitly export a chart or report to your photo library.

XM Mint does not request access to Contacts, Microphone, Location, Calendars, Reminders, Health, Motion, Face ID, Speech Recognition, Bluetooth, or Local Network.

5. Network requests

If you enable price refresh, XM Mint may send requests to public market data providers to fetch displayed price points for assets you have added. These requests do not include your device identifier, advertising ID, or any persistent identifier linking the request to you as a person. They contain only the symbol you added.

You can disable network refresh at any time in Settings; the App will continue to function with the most recently cached values.

6. Support emails

If you email us at huelmelissa1841991@gmail.com, we receive only the information you include in your message. We use it solely to respond to your request, retain it only as long as needed for that purpose, and never share it with third parties for marketing.

7. Children

XM Mint is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. International users

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no cross-border transfer of your portfolio information. Standard email replies are sent from the email service the developer uses; if you are in the EEA, UK, Switzerland or California and wish to exercise rights (access, deletion, portability), contact us at huelmelissa1841991@gmail.com and we will respond.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or to applicable law. Material changes will be announced in-app and reflected by an updated "Effective date" above. Continued use of XM Mint after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
