Tracking Disclosure

This section explains the data XM Mint collects, in plain language.

XM Mint does not track you. The app does not use any third-party analytics SDK, advertising SDK, attribution SDK, or fingerprinting technology. We do not collect device identifiers (such as IDFA, IDFV, or GAID), advertising IDs, IP addresses for tracking purposes, cookies, or web beacons. We do not record your interactions, taps, screens visited, sessions, or crash reports in a way that can identify you or your device.

All portfolio, transaction, receipt and image data you create in XM Mint is stored locally on your device using the app's private sandbox. The developer cannot access this data, and it is never transmitted to any server operated by the developer or any third party.

The only network requests XM Mint may make are to publicly available market data endpoints used to refresh displayed price information for assets you have manually added. These requests do not contain personal identifiers and are not used to track users. You can disable price refresh at any time in Settings.

If you choose to contact support by email at huelmelissa1841991@gmail.com, we will receive only the information you voluntarily include in your message. We use it solely to respond to your request and delete it once it is no longer needed.

XM Mint does not access your contacts, microphone, calendar, location, health data, or any iCloud-synced content beyond what iOS itself may surface to user-installed apps in general.

If we ever introduce a feature that requires tracking (for example, optional cloud backup or crash reporting), we will update this disclosure, update the Privacy Policy, and request your consent before any tracking begins, in full compliance with the App Store Tracking policy and applicable privacy laws.
